# Extra programmable keys, repairability (RAM/SSD/parts), and docking with two displays on Linux

## Answer
Almost no mainstream 15-16 in laptop has built-in macro keys. The Framework Laptop 16 is the one real exception: its keyboard, numpad and macropad modules run QMK firmware and can be remapped with VIA (Vial builds exist for the macropad). Everywhere else the practical route is a full-size keyboard with a numpad (for example ThinkPad T16 / P16 class) remapped in software with keyd or Input Remapper, plus a small external QMK/VIA USB macro pad if six dedicated keys are wanted. That fallback is sensible but is inferred, not tested here. On repairability the ThinkPad T-series 16 in is the best-documented business option: iFixit 10/10 for the T14 Gen 7 / T16 Gen 5 (2026), with RAM and SSD upgradeable; P16s Gen 5 is 9/10; the Framework Laptop 16 also scores 10/10. The Framework 16 has two SODIMM slots and two M.2 slots but no confirmed cellular option and no confirmed Israel shipping, which clashes with tier 1 (eSIM). The ThinkPad P16 Gen 3 has 4 SODIMM slots, 3 M.2 slots, optional 5G WWAN, a numpad and Thunderbolt 5/4, so it is the strongest tier 1 / tier 3 fit on paper, but it is a heavy workstation. RAM is expensive in late 2026 (about $15-16.5/GB for loose DDR5 SO-DIMM), so the "upgrade later" saving is much smaller than before; Framework's own option prices ($405 for 32 GB, $810 for 64 GB) show the OEM does not undercut the market. For docking, a Thunderbolt 4 dock with documented Linux support (Dell WD22TB4 or Lenovo 40B0) is the safest pick for Ethernet plus two displays; AMD hosts were not covered by the vendor testing found.

## Disagreements and gaps
- Numeric iFixit scores are not in the fetched text; 9/10 (P16s G5) and Framework 10/10 rest on secondary articles. The iFixit page I read was fully 2026 for T14 G7/T16 G5 and P16s G5 but gave only prose.
- Not confirmed from a primary source: T16 Gen 5 AMD uses SODIMM while Intel uses LPCAMM2 (only from a retailer/guide snippet); M.2 slot count on T16 Gen 5 and P16s Gen 5; whether T16 Gen 5 has a numpad; Dell Pro 16 / HP ZBook 8 G2a 16 slot counts and repairability scores (searches found none).
- Framework store and docs pages returned HTTP 403 (Cloudflare) to WebFetch, curl and the server browser; Framework facts come from Phoronix, the Framework community and one cached page, not frame.work product pages. Whether the Framework 16 can take a WWAN/eSIM module, and whether Framework ships to Israel, was not confirmed (a forum thread snippet said it had not launched in Israel; undated, unread in full).
- No Israel-specific data found for ThinkPad spare parts or service centres (only a German reseller listing a Hebrew-layout Lenovo keyboard part, snippet only). Israeli RAM prices not checked; the US-dollar SO-DIMM prices are from a tracker, not retail quotes.
- Whether the macropad/VIA route is usable on Ubuntu without udev rules, and which external macro pad suits PTT, was not researched; recommended as a fallback by inference only.
- No dock was confirmed with dual displays plus Ethernet on an AMD laptop under Linux; AMD host behaviour over Thunderbolt/USB4 MST is not documented in the sources read. Docking sources are review sites, not tests by independent labs.
- RAM trend: the "RAM later vs built-in" comparison lacks an OEM upgrade price list for the shortlisted ThinkPads; only Framework's pricing was seen, and for an out-of-stock 7040 configuration.

## Confidence
Medium for repairability scores and the Framework/ThinkPad slot facts (primary iFixit page plus spec sheet); Low for docking recommendations on AMD and for anything about Israel (parts, shipping, prices), because those were not found or only appeared in search snippets.
