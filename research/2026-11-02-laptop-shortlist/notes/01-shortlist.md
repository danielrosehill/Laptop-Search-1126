# 01-shortlist: Which current 15-16 in laptops meet tier 1 (32-64 GB, 2 TB, eSIM/WWAN 5G, Wi-Fi 7, USB-C charging, durable keyboard)?

## Answer
Lenovo is the only vendor where I confirmed every tier-1 item on a manufacturer spec sheet. The ThinkPad T16 Gen 5 (Intel, Panther Lake; PSREF dated 2026-09-21) takes up to 64 GB of user-replaceable LPCAMM2, a 2 TB PCIe 5.0 SSD, Wi-Fi 7 (BE211), a Snapdragon X61 5G modem with eSIM, and USB-C charging. The ThinkPad P16s Gen 5 (AMD Ryzen AI PRO 400 or Intel Panther Lake H) is the stronger-CPU option: up to 96 GB LPCAMM2, 2 TB, 5G eSIM, Wi-Fi 7, and Ubuntu is a preload option. The HP EliteBook 8 G1i 16 (SODIMM, 64 GB, 2 TB, 5G, Wi-Fi 7; QuickSpecs 2026-08-18) also qualifies on paper. Its newer Panther Lake siblings (EliteBook 8 G2i 16 and 6 G2i 16) are advertised with 64 GB and 5G, but I could not read their spec sheets. The Dell Pro 16 Plus orders with a 5G tray, but the Dell pages I read show at most 32 GB with 5G, and Dell's own site gave me no 64 GB/2 TB/5G configuration. The Framework Laptop 16 has no cellular option that I could find, so it fails tier 1. I found no manufacturer list price for any of the exact 32 GB/2 TB/5G or 64 GB/2 TB/5G configurations; the prices below are base prices and a few full-configuration retailer prices, all with dates.

### Claims and sources
- T16 Gen 5 Intel: one LPCAMM2 slot up to 64 GB, one M.2 2280 PCIe 5.0 slot up to 2 TB, Intel BE211 Wi-Fi 7, 5 MP camera options with privacy shutter, dual-microphone array, and a Snapdragon X61 5G Sub-6 modem with embedded eSIM; keyboard is 6-row, spill-resistant, with an LED backlight option; Ubuntu is a listed preload [S0101].
- T16 Gen 5 AMD: Gorgon Point allows up to 96 GB DDR5-5600 SODIMM and 2 TB; Hawk Point caps at 64 GB and 1 TB; X61 5G eSIM listed [S0102].
- T16 Gen 5 in North America: Intel only, with 16/32/64 GB LPCAMM2 at 8,533 MT/s, optional 5G, base $1,628 (US), published 2026-05-12 [S0111].
- P16s Gen 5 AMD: up to 96 GB LPCAMM2, 2 TB Gen 5 SSD, X61 5G eSIM, MT7925 Wi-Fi 7, Ubuntu LTS preload listed [S0103]. Intel variant: Panther Lake H, up to 96 GB, 2 TB, X61 5G eSIM, BE211 Wi-Fi 7 [S0104].
- P16s i Gen 5 US launch SKU: Core Ultra 7 356H, RTX PRO 500, 32 GB LPCAMM2, 1 TB, 90 Wh, from $3,069; more configurations to come [S0112].
- HP EliteBook 8 G1i 16 AI PC: 2x SODIMM to 64 GB, 2 TB SSD options, BE201 Wi-Fi 7, MediaTek T700 5G, "WWAN-ready configuration must be selected at purchase to add WWAN later", backlit spill-resistant DuraKey keyboard (QuickSpecs v21, 2026-08-18) [S0107]. The Next Gen variant is soldered to 32 GB [S0108].
- HP EliteBook 6 G2i 16: Panther Lake, up to 64 GB, 2 TB PCIe 5.0, 5G, US from $2,732 [S0109]. 8 G2i 16: up to 64 GB, Germany from EUR 2,133 [S0110].
- Dell US store (read in a server browser, 2026-11-02): 5G WWAN tray offered; Intel BE201 Wi-Fi 7; backlit keyboard; 32 GB is the highest memory shown; prices as in the table [S0113]. The owner's manual found is for the Core Ultra 200V variant [S0114].
- All sources above for Lenovo are PSREF PDFs fetched directly from psref.lenovo.com (Lenovo's manufacturer spec reference).
- Keyboard durability: all Lenovo and HP business lines listed use spill-resistant keyboards (stated in specs). I did not find a durability test for any of them; "durable" is inferred from the business-line class and the keyboard-replacement repairability that sub-question 3 covers.

## Disagreements and gaps
- No source showed a single orderable SKU that combines 64 GB + 2 TB + 5G for any model, and I found no list price for any such SKU. Lenovo PSREF lists each as a separate option; the configurator was not queried (lenovo.com and frame.work returned 403 to scripted fetches). Treat "orderable together" as inferred.
- Lenovo's PSREF shows WWAN is per-SKU (the T16 Gen 4 SKU 21QE007TUS has "WWAN Not Supported"). HP says WWAN-ready hardware must be chosen at purchase. Both match the brief's assumption that eSIM is a purchase-time choice.
- Dell: the manufacturer page showed 32 GB max for 5G configurations, while one retailer snippet (Kuwait) listed 64 GB/1 TB/WWAN on an Ultra 7 255U. The retailer page 404ed when I fetched it, so it is unverified.
- Prices mix currencies and dates (US list, EU incl. VAT, AUD retail). None are Israeli prices; that belongs to sub-question 4. US pages were reached from an Israeli IP and may differ from what a US shopper sees.
- Unchecked: HP 8 G2i 16 memory type (soldered vs SODIMM), P16 Gen 3 webcam/mic/backlight, the Wi-Fi chip on Framework Laptop 16, whether the Israeli Lenovo/HP/Dell channels sell any WWAN SKUs at all.
- Did not cover the ThinkPad X1 Carbon/X1 lines (14 in, out of the 15-16 in range), HP ZBook, or other brands (Asus ProArt/ExpertBook, Acer, Samsung). A gap, not a finding of absence.

## Confidence
Medium. The Lenovo and HP G1i specs are from manufacturer documents dated within three months and agree with press coverage. Confidence is lower for the exact orderable 64 GB + 2 TB + 5G combinations, for all prices (base prices only), and for the Dell, HP G2i and Framework rows, which rest partly on retailer or press pages.
