# Prices and channel: what do the shortlisted configurations cost in Israel vs. abroad, and how do warranties work?

## Answer
In Israel on 2026-11-02, a 16 in business laptop with 32 GB RAM and a cellular modem is available, but 2 TB is not. The closest match found is the ThinkPad T16 Gen 4 21QE0043IV (Core Ultra 7 255U, 32 GB, 1 TB, 4G LTE) at about 9,350 to 10,938 ILS including VAT. Adding a 2 TB NVMe drive (Samsung 990 Pro 2TB from 1,536 ILS) brings a basic spec to roughly 11,000 to 12,500 ILS. A 64 GB / 2 TB 16 in business laptop with WWAN was not found in Israeli stock. The nearest 64 GB models are the ThinkPad P16s Gen 5 (64 GB / 1 TB / RTX PRO, 18,012 to 18,960 ILS) and the Dell Pro 5 Series 16 (U7 355, 64 GB / 1 TB, 13,894 ILS, WWAN not stated). A stretch spec therefore sits around 15,000 to 19,000 ILS locally. US list prices are lower: a T16 Gen 5 with 32 GB / 1 TB lists at USD 2,609 (about 8,160 ILS at 3.1284, before US sales tax and before Israeli VAT), but the exact US/Israeli SKUs differ, so the saving was not measured like for like. Lenovo's International Warranty Service lets a US-bought ThinkPad be serviced in Israel only if that machine type is sold and supported there, and the base term is the US one (often 1 year). Dell and HP rules are weaker or unverified. Framework could not be confirmed as shipping to Israel (the only evidence is a 2023 note that it had not launched there). The most defensible channel is Israel for a spec with WWAN, with the SSD upgraded locally, unless the visit to the US lets him carry a unit whose machine type Lenovo Israel also sells.

### Import cost
- Israeli VAT is 18% (since 2025-01-01) [S0416]. The personal-import customs-free threshold is reported variously as USD 75, USD 150 or USD 500 in secondary and older sources; none of these is confirmed from the Tax Authority, whose calculator page loaded but its calculator did not [S0415][S0418]. The same sources say computers are customs-duty exempt and the VAT applies to the invoice value (secondary, partially dated).
- Practical estimate (inferred): a laptop bought in the US for USD 2,600 and brought in costs 18% VAT on top, about 1,470 ILS, if declared; carrying it in luggage "as personal effects" without declaring is not a plan to rely on.

### Warranty
- Lenovo IWS: covers any IWS-eligible machine type in any country where that machine type is announced, sold and supported; no registration required; the length is that of the country of origin; on-site service is delivered where available; refurbished machines are excluded; software, sealed battery, ADP and KYD upgrades have limits [S0409][S0410]. The Lenovo Israel page is the exact policy text. Eligibility is per 4-digit machine type, so he should look up the machine type on Lenovo's IWS lookup before buying; a US-only machine type is not covered in Israel.
- A search snippet attributed to the IWS FAQ says customers needing IWS in Israel may be asked for proof of purchase and importation documents; I did not find this sentence on the FAQ page I read [S0410], so treat as unconfirmed.
- Israeli importer behaviour: Zap seller text for the T16 Gen 4 says its 3-year warranty is on site in Israel only [S0401]. No source found says Lenovo Israel's importer refuses IWS claims; no source found says it honours them either. Unconfirmed.
- Dell: forum posts (Dell community, undated or old) say international service depends on having bought in-home/on-site service in the US, on the model also being sold in Israel, and on a service-tag transfer; there is a paid Dell International Services program [S0413]. No current official Dell page was retrieved (the Dell warranty URL I tried 404'd).
- HP: the HP Worldwide Limited Warranty text says it may be enforced where HP or its authorised providers offer service for the same product model number; forums report local providers refusing imported units [S0414]. Not Israel-specific.
- Framework: community thread dated 2023-08-08 says Framework had not launched in Israel, forwarding addresses may cause order cancellation, and workarounds are not permitted [S0411]. frame.work itself returned HTTP 403 / a Cloudflare challenge in both WebFetch and the browser, so current shipping status is unconfirmed. Zap has no Framework listings (search "framework laptop 16" returned zero results) [S0419].

## Disagreements and gaps
- No Israeli 16 in business laptop at 2 TB or at 64 GB / 2 TB with WWAN was found. KSP blocked fetch (403) and its search page returned 404 in the browser; Ivory and Bug fetched via curl but I did not extract model prices from them; Zap aggregates many of the same resellers but is not KSP.
- Zap shows WWAN only where the listing title says so (T16 Gen 4 21QE0043IV, "4G LTE"). eSIM support, 5G and antenna provisioning are not stated for most SKUs. A one-line check of Lenovo PSREF or the importer is needed per SKU.
- Prices on Zap are the asking prices of resellers on one day; lowest values may be out of stock. VAT inclusion is inferred.
- US list prices: only Lenovo US was read; Dell and HP US pages did not load product data. The US configurations are not the same SKUs as the Israeli ones (different CPU, touch). Street prices unknown.
- Customs/import thresholds conflict across secondary sources (USD 75 / 150 / 500); the official calculator was not usable.
- Framework Israel shipping status after 2023 is unconfirmed; Framework price for 64 GB / 2 TB is not found.
- Dell and HP international-warranty evidence is forum-level, not official.

## Confidence
Medium for Israeli ILS price ranges (read directly from Zap on 2026-11-02, with VAT inclusion inferred) — Low for US-versus-Israel savings, import duty/VAT thresholds, Dell/HP/Framework warranty and Framework shipping, because those rest on one list-price source, secondary sources or old forum posts.
